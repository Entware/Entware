    rockchip: enable the RV1126 RGA
    
    The RGA node of the RV1126 was added disabled, and no board enables
    it, so the rockchip-rga module loads without a device to bind. The
    engine has no pins or external interfaces, so like the RGA nodes of
    the other Rockchip SoCs it carries no status property and is enabled
    by the SoC dtsi. The driver binds through the rk3288-rga fallback
    compatible of the node; the dts patches that follow the node are
    re-contexted.
